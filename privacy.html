Crow · Privacy policy

Last updated: 25.10.2026 · AeroShift Games

In short

Everything you write in Crow is stored only on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools. We cannot see your entries, we don’t sell them and we don’t share them with anyone. The app only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore Crow Pro.

Data controller and this notice

The data controller is AeroShift Games, the developer of Crow (contact: oztuk.4er@gmail.com). This text is the information notice under Article 10 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the information required by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the privacy policy required by Google Play’s User Data policy.

What stays on your phone

Your entries (reminders, medicines, payments, installments, habits, water, expenses, notes, lists), their history and your settings. They live in the app’s private storage on your phone (Android Preferences); they are never sent to a server and we cannot see them. Medicine entries may be health-related, so we never take them off your phone. They leave it only when you choose to (backup file, spreadsheet export, sharing) or through Android’s automatic backup if it is on.

Purchases (Google Play)

Crow Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. The app asks Google Play directly whether you own Pro, using the purchase record in your Google account (product, date, transaction ID). Your name, email or anything you write in Crow is not sent to us or any other company. Card and payment details stay with Google; we cannot see them. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications and permissions

Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; their content never passes through a server. The notification permission is requested so reminders can be shown. The “Alarms & reminders” permission is optional and makes reminders arrive right on the minute. Crow sends no ads or promotional notifications.

Voice input

The microphone button opens your phone’s system speech recognition; Crow doesn’t ask for the microphone permission. Speech-to-text is done by your phone’s recognition provider (usually Google) under its own terms. Crow keeps no audio; it only receives the resulting text and stores it on your phone.

Backups

If Android auto backup is on, Android may back up Crow’s data to your Google account (encrypted) and restore it on a new phone. This backup is managed by Android and Google and we can’t access it; you can turn it off in your phone’s Settings → Google → Backup. When you use “Create a backup file”, you decide where the file goes.

Home screen widget

The widget hides amounts by default (•••). Widget data is kept only on your phone.

Purposes and legal bases

Purchase data is processed to provide and verify Pro (KVKK Art. 5/2-c, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b): entering into and performing a contract). It is processed automatically through Google Play. Your data is never sold or used for ads or profiling.

Transfers and other countries

Purchases are handled by Google Play (Google); Google’s servers may be outside your country, including in the USA. Such transfers rely on the safeguards in KVKK Art. 9 and GDPR Chapter V (e.g. standard contractual clauses). Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). We never transfer the entries you write in Crow anywhere.

How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google keeps the purchase record in your Google account and for as long as legal (e.g. tax) obligations require.

Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 and the GDPR you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to request information, correction and deletion, to object to processing, to learn which third parties receive it and to claim compensation for damage. Send requests to oztuk.4er@gmail.com; we reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. You can complain to Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Board or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.

Children

Crow is not directed at children under 13; it is designed for ages 13 and up. It doesn’t ask for your name, email or ID details. If you believe data of a child under 13 has been processed, write to us and we’ll delete it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Backup & data → “Delete all data” to erase everything on your phone; uninstalling the app also removes all Crow data. We keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing else to delete on our side. For questions or requests, email oztuk.4er@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this text, we update its date and announce important changes inside the app together with an app update. If a new kind of data processing needs your permission, we ask before it starts.

Contact

Questions, requests and suggestions: oztuk.4er@gmail.com (AeroShift Games).
